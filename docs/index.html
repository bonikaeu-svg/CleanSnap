❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
How does CleanSnap remove GPS and metadata offline?

CleanSnap executes all parsing and scrubbing directly inside your device hardware via HTML5 Canvas (browser), Android MediaStore/MediaMuxer (mobile), or Python/FFmpeg (Windows). Files never touch an external server.

Does video cleaning reduce visual or audio quality?

No. CleanSnap performs lossless container stream copying (native stream copy). It strips the metadata atoms (such as moov and creation tags) without re-encoding the video or audio streams.

What photo and video formats are supported?

Photos: JPEG (.jpg, .jpeg), PNG (.png), WebP (.webp), and iPhone HEIC/HEIF (.heic). Videos: MP4 (.mp4), QuickTime MOV (.mov), and MKV (.mkv).

Is my privacy protected?

Yes, 100%. CleanSnap has zero tracking, zero telemetry, no advertisements, and zero cloud uploads. The full source code is publicly inspectable on GitHub.
