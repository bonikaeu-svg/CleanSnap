# Privacy Policy for CleanSnap

**Last updated:** October 2026

CleanSnap ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles your data.

### 1. Zero Data Collection
CleanSnap is built as a 100% private, offline utility.
* **No Personal Data Collected:** We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information.
* **No Account Required:** You do not need an account, email address, or phone number to use CleanSnap.
* **No Trackers or Analytics:** CleanSnap contains zero third-party analytics SDKs, advertising libraries, or telemetry trackers.

### 2. Media Processing & Storage
* All photo and video processing (EXIF stripping, GPS removal, and container cleaning) takes place **entirely on your device**.
* Your photos and videos are **never uploaded** to the cloud or transferred over the internet.
* Cleaned media files are saved locally to your device's standard storage directory (`Pictures/Cleaned_Media` and `Movies/Cleaned_Media`).

### 3. Permissions
CleanSnap uses modern Android system pickers (Photo Picker and MediaStore APIs):
* We do **not** request broad storage permissions or access your entire file system.
* The app only accesses the specific photos and videos you explicitly choose to clean.

### 4. Children's Privacy
Because CleanSnap collects no personal information whatsoever, it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

### 5. Contact
If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach out via GitHub or project issue tracker.
